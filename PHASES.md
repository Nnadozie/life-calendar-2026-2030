## Glossary

The vocabulary the plan uses. If a term in the calendar is unfamiliar, it is here.

- **Open Studies** — UCalgary's unclassified-student status: you take for-credit courses without yet being admitted to a degree program. Phase 1–3 sit here.
- **Undergraduate Admission Guarantee** — The real route into BSc Computer Science: 18 Open Studies units + GPA ≥ 2.50 (Science) + English 30-1. (The old '10-course Open Studies' rule is a myth — do not plan around it.)
- **Declare the major** — The change-of-program request that moves you from Open Studies into the BSc Computer Science program once the admission guarantee is met (Phase 4).
- **Science Co-op** — UCalgary's co-op program. Admission windows are fixed (Fall Sep–Oct / Winter Jan–Feb); you must be *admitted*, then complete the Co-op Prep Program, then secure a position — all within one year of admission.
- **Work term** — A paid, full-time, degree-relevant placement (e.g. Summer 2028, Beijing). Work terms must align to academic semesters.
- **CLIC** — A Chinese-language study-abroad network; the exchange runs through it to host institutions such as Tsinghua / BLCU.
- **HSK** — Hanyu Shuiping Kaoshi — the standard Chinese Proficiency Test. HSK 4 ≈ intermediate, HSK 5 ≈ upper-intermediate. UCalgary has no official CHIN→HSK mapping, so levels here are honest estimates.
- **CHIN 205 / 207 / 301 / 303** — The UCalgary Chinese sequence. By CHIN 303 you are working at roughly HSK 4 level.
- **Blind 75 / NeetCode 150** — Curated LeetCode problem sets used as interview preparation. The plan masters them repeatedly: pass 1 slow, then timed passes.
- **ROS2** — Robot Operating System 2 — the robotics middleware (nodes, topics, services, launch files) the portfolio is built on.
- **Gazebo** — An open-source robotics simulator — the entry point before GPU sim.
- **Isaac Sim** — NVIDIA's GPU robotics simulation platform, used for humanoid simulation and synthetic sensor data.
- **VLM** — Vision-language model — a model that reasons over images and text together. Project 1 maps a VLM's output to robot joint targets.
- **Capstone** — The final-year project: VLM-driven humanoid manipulation, built Jan–Mar 2030 and defended April 2030.
- **Alberta Income Support** — Provincial income assistance for Sep–Dec 2026 (~$600 CAD/mo, UNVERIFIED). Requires monthly reports and kept bank statements.
- **Alberta Student Aid** — Provincial student loans/grants. To stay eligible from Jan 2027 you must keep full-time status (≥ 9 units) or an approved co-op term.
- **Critical Path lane** — The lane at the top of every week: the application or meeting *due*, the enabling action to *start now* with its lead time, and the horizon. This lane is what sequences the plan.
- **Gate** — A phase's criteria-based definition of done. A phase ends when its gate is TRUE, not when a date passes; the gate is the prerequisite for the next phase.
- **Leading indicator** — One measurable weekly signal (e.g. problems solved, characters learned, commits pushed) that tells you progress as data, not vibes.
- **Z visa / work permit** — The Chinese visa and work-permit documents required for the co-op term (Phase 6) — start the paperwork in Phase 5.
- **Transfer credit** — Course credit earned on exchange that must be approved and applied to your UCalgary transcript (filed during/after the exchange year).
